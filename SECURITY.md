# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose a suspected vulnerability in a public issue.

Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/tutao0123/open-linkage/security/advisories/new) to share:

- the affected page, feature, or revision
- steps to reproduce
- the potential impact
- any suggested mitigation

You should receive an initial response within seven days. A fix and disclosure timeline will depend on severity and reproducibility.

## Engineering disclaimer

OpenLinkage is an early-stage mechanism design and educational tool. Generated mechanisms and numerical results must be independently checked before fabrication or use in any safety-critical application.
